About:

Horticulture is quite the recent hobby for me, having started at the beginning of summer. My aunt wanted to plant a variety of different flora, and she wished for my help; thus, we did, and it's been quite the interesting hobby thus far.


Thus far we've successfully grown and maintained:
  • Tomatoes, specifically:
    • Beefmaster
    • Two varieties of Cherry Tomato, one red and one orange
  • Two varieties of Lettuce
  • Celery
  • White and pink flowers, planted in our front yard
  • Geraniums, vibrant red flowers
  • Calibrachoa, little flowers that come in a lot of colors
  • Marigold flowers

As someone with an interest in Horticulture, my aunt and I have gone to the local landscaping and greenhouse building, Massis, for general adivce and a plethora of plants and supplies. Given that this was a summer hobby, it also bore fruit, quite literally, with my aunt and parents managing to harvest several dozen cherry tomatoes and a good few Beefmasters.
