About yellowstone:

The very first thing you should know about Yellowstone is that it is not a "supervolcano," in fact that term is effectively meaningless.
What Yellowstone actually is, is a "caldera," a large underground reservoir of mostly-solid magma, capable of producing "supereruptions."

The park itself is quite large and consists of two primary loops and five entrances.
Though it may not seem like it, most parts of Yellowstone are quite a large distance from each other, and given the fairly low speed limit of approximately 35mph (56 km/h), it's practically impossible to see everything in a single day (hence a four day trip).


One of the multitude of signs encountered in this park

Signs are a very common thing in Yellowstone, and for a good reason. Dozens of people have lost their lives in the park.
